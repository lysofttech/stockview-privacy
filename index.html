StockView — Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 11, 2026

This privacy policy applies to the StockView mobile application (package com.lysofttech.stockview) developed by LYSoft Technologies ("we", "us").

1. Summary

StockView does not collect, store, or share any personal data. The app has no user accounts, no sign-in, no analytics SDKs, and no advertising.

2. Information stored on your device

Your watchlist, display preferences (such as chart type and sort order), and a cache of recently fetched market prices are stored only in the app's private storage on your device. This information never leaves your device and is deleted when you uninstall the app.

3. Network requests

To show market data, the app requests publicly available stock price information from third-party market data sources (the Pakistan Stock Exchange data portal and Yahoo Finance). These requests contain only the stock symbols being looked up. As with any internet request, the servers you contact receive your IP address as part of normal network operation; we do not receive, log, or have access to any of this. The respective providers' terms and privacy policies apply to their services.

4. Data we collect

None. We do not collect personal information, usage analytics, device identifiers, location data, or any other data about you.

5. Data sharing and selling

We do not share or sell any data, because we do not collect any.

6. Children

The app does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children. The app is intended for a general audience and displays financial market information.

7. Not financial advice

Market data shown in the app is for informational purposes only, may be delayed or inaccurate, and does not constitute investment advice.

8. Changes to this policy

If a future version of the app changes any of the above (for example, by adding analytics), this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the "Last updated" date will change.
